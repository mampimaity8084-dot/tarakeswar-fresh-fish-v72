🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

1. Information we collect

Name, mobile number, delivery area/address, order details and necessary payment references.

2. Why we use it

To process orders, deliver products, verify payments, provide support, rewards and account services.

3. Payments

Payment credentials are processed by the configured payment provider. Do not store sensitive payment credentials in the app database.

4. Location

Live location may be used for active delivery tracking. Tracking should stop when the delivery is completed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by necessary service providers such as hosting, payment, messaging and maps.

6. Security

Server secrets must be stored in Cloudflare environment variables and never exposed in browser code.

7. Requests

For correction or deletion requests, contact support through the app.
